# Content safety for uploads (binding, after the 2026-10-27 account moderation)

**What happened.** After the city-art batch, 115 images uploaded in about 5 minutes, every Open Cloud call for the owner account returned `403 "User is moderated"`. The batch included flyers with (fictional) phone numbers, a photoreal political campaign poster ("RE-ELECT MAYOR …"), and several photoreal AI humans. The owner must read the notice on roblox.com to learn the actual trigger.

## Rules for every image, model, audio or text we upload or show in-game

1. **No contact info of any kind.** No phone numbers (including 555 numbers), emails, URLs, social handles or addresses that look real.
2. **No political content.** No campaigns, elections, politicians, parties, protest or political slogans. A fictional "mayor" is still political.
3. **No photoreal people in uploaded images.** Use stylized illustration, silhouettes, or no people at all. Never use real people or celebrity look-alikes.
4. **No real brands, logos, trademarks or GTA/Rockstar names.** Use parody brands only.
5. **No alcohol, tobacco, vaping, drugs or pharmacy drug names.** No gambling imagery: no casino, dice, slots, cards, betting or lottery.
6. **No gore or blood, no sexualized content, no gang signs or real gang names, no hate symbols, no realistic weapon advertising on billboards.**
7. **Text in images must be short, fictional and clean.** Keep it free of profanity, slurs, and anything that could be read as an off-platform invite.
8. **Upload pacing.** Send at most 1 upload every 10 s and at most 60 per hour. Pre-screen every image with a review agent against these rules **before** upload, and stop the batch on the first rejection.
9. **Third-party models** (Creator Store, Sketchfab) get scripts stripped, logos and badges removed, and are checked for embedded text and decals before upload.
