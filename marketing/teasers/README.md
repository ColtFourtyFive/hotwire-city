# Teaser clips

These are 5-second, 1280×720, 24 fps clips with audio. They were generated with Higgsfield `seedance_2_5` from the thumbnail art in `marketing/`. They are concept teasers for social posts and the experience page video slot, not gameplay footage.

| File | Shot | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `teaser_reel.mp4` | The chase clip, crossfaded into the hot-drop clip (9.6 s) | Ready to post |
| `teaser_chase.mp4` | A purple supercar jumps a ramp on nitro while cash flies, police give chase and a helicopter follows | Ready to post |
| `teaser_hot_drop.mp4` | The server-wide Hot Drop beacon | Ready to post |
| `teaser_stolen.mp4` | The "your car is being stolen" moment | **Do not post as-is.** It inherits a "ROBLOX" sign and a supercar badge from `thumb_4_stolen.png`. Re-cut it from an in-game capture. |

Roblox requires experience-page media to represent the game. Once the place is live, replace these with real in-game captures, or put them alongside them.
